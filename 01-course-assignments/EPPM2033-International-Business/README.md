## Assignment

**Title**: A Comparative Analysis of McDonald's Corporation in the United States and Malaysia

This individual assignment examines how McDonald’s balances global standardization with local responsiveness (a “glocal” strategy). It applies concepts of globalization motives and Hofstede’s cultural dimensions to compare the U.S. and Malaysian markets, and ends with a practical recommendation for managing the efficiency–local relevance tension.

### Key sections
1. Company Overview  
2. Globalization drivers and entry  
3. Cultural Environment (Hofstede comparison)  
4. Cultural Adaptation (U.S. vs Malaysia examples)  
5. Conclusion and Recommendation (Local Innovation Board proposal)
